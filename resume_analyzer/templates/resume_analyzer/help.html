Welcome to ResuRank
Every score has a reason. You make every final decision.
1
Create a job

Go to Jobs and click New Job. Add the title, department and location. Add the required skills and the preferred skills. Use the weight slider to choose how much the score depends on skills and how much on resume similarity.

Tip: You can save as a draft and come back later.

2
Publish the job

A job needs at least one required skill to be published. Click Publish. Only active jobs accept applications. You can close a job at any time.

3
Share the apply link

Open your job and copy the apply link. Send it to candidates. They do not need to log in. After applying, each candidate gets a tracking link to see their status.

You can also upload a resume yourself from Analyze Resume.

4
View applicants

Open a job and click Applicants. You will see a ranked table with the score, matched skills and status of each person. Use the filters to find people quickly.

5
Read the analysis

Click a candidate to open the analysis. You will see the score ring, the score formula, matched and missing skills (click a skill for the reason), resume quality, and resume parts.

Names, gender and photos are never used in scoring.

6
Decide and compare

Use Shortlist, Move to Review or Reject on the analysis page. ResuRank never rejects anyone by itself. To compare, open Compare and pick 2 to 4 candidates to see them side by side.

7
Check analytics

Open Analytics to see applications per week, status breakdown, score distribution, the most missing skills, and how each job is performing.

Human decisions. Better informed.
